Learning

When help is everywhere, how do we learn to help ourselves?

I’m interested in what learners do before, during, and after a task: setting goals, checking their understanding, and deciding what to try next.

AI & people

What should AI make easier—and what is worth the effort?

An easier task is not always a better learning experience. I’m curious about how educational AI can support understanding while leaving space for learners’ own judgment.

Knowledge & trust

What makes a piece of information worth trusting?

Who speaks, how a message is shared, and what others say can shape our judgments. I study how these social and technical cues relate to trust and knowledge sharing.
