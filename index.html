EDUCATION, TECHNOLOGY & THE HUMAN SIDE

Learning, with

Hi, I’m Jun. I explore how we learn, what we trust, and where AI fits into the picture.

I study in the field of educational technology at East China Normal University. My interests connect self-regulated learning, educational AI, and the ways people share and interpret knowledge.

Research atlas

Different paths.
A connected curiosity.

My research moves between people, learning, and the systems around them. This is how those interests connect.

Research directions

Learning & AI+

Learning with support, thinking for ourselves.

I explore how learners plan, monitor, and reflect—and how AI can support those processes while leaving room for their own judgment.

  • Self-regulated learning
  • Educational agents
  • AI literacy
  • Human–AI interaction
Knowledge & trust+

From information to understanding.

I’m interested in how people interpret information, decide what to trust, and share what they know across social and digital spaces.

  • Information processing
  • Knowledge sharing
  • Multimodal communication
  • Trust
Teachers & communities+

The people around the learner.

Learning also takes shape through relationships. I study mentoring, teachers’ professional development, and the communities that sustain participation.

  • Mentoring
  • Professional capital
  • Teacher development
  • Educational leadership
Digital society+

Widening the lens to systems.

Beyond individual learning, I examine how digital technologies and public policy relate to organisational resilience and innovation.

  • Digital transformation
  • Resilience
  • Urban innovation
  • Policy evaluation
Ways of looking

Evidence synthesis / Learning analytics / Empirical modelling
